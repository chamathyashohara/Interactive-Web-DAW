  E2 same as E after the fix                                      PASS

  G  producer closes the session mid-take                         PASS after fix (performer stops+saves)

Defects found by these runs and fixed in the same change:
  1. (E) Producer: a STOP_SCHEDULE that could not be sent because the link was down was never
     re-sent; the performer kept recording until the session closed. Fix: the stop is "owed" and
     re-sent with a new stop time when the performer reconnects (performer take ends later; E2
     shows producer 155962 frames vs performer 163405 frames, reported honestly).
  2. (E) Performer: SESSION_CLOSED while Recording left the take open. Fix: when the link ends for
     good (Disconnected) during Recording, the performer stops and saves its own take.
  3. (G, found by a new unit test) ControlLink::stop closed the socket right after SESSION_CLOSED;
     with unread heartbeats in the receive buffer Windows sends RST (WSA 10054), destroying
     SESSION_CLOSED. Fix: half-close + drain up to 500 ms before closing.
