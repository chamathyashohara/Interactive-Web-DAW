================================================================================================

digital near-silence (about -160 dBFS), so nothing audible is claimed.
Build: core-check-gcc (MinGW g++ 16.2), NOT the MSVC product build; Qt GUI not compiled.
Set-up: TWO PROBE PROCESSES ON ONE PC. Both record the SAME physical input device (WASAPI shared mode);
the transfer runs over loopback (127.0.0.1, control port + 2). This is NOT the two-PC acceptance test.

then "transfer 1". Fault injection uses the probe's TEST-ONLY options named in each command line.
Pairing codes are replaced by <redacted>. Transfer tokens are never printed or logged by the apps.
